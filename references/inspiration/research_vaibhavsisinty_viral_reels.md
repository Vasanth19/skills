## 4. Formats That Work Best

### Primary Format: Talking Head (60-70% of content)
- Direct-to-camera, confident delivery
- Minimal set — clean background or branded backdrop
- Text overlays reinforcing key points
- Quick cuts between sentences (removes dead air)
- Captions always on (74% retention boost)

### Secondary Format: Screen Recording + Voiceover (20-25%)
- Live AI tool demos (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity)
- Screen recordings with cursor tracking
- Voiceover explaining what's happening
- Text callouts highlighting key features

### Tertiary Format: Text-Heavy Listicles (5-10%)
- Numbered lists with bold text overlays
- Minimal or no face-cam
- Background music/trending audio
- Fast-paced transitions between points

### Editing Style:
- **Fast-paced** — 2-3 second cuts, no dead air
- **Text overlays** on every key point (not just captions — graphic text)
- **Zoom cuts** for emphasis on important statements
- **Clean typography** — bold, readable, high contrast
- **No fancy transitions** — simple cuts, occasional zoom
- **Music:** Subtle background, trending audio when appropriate
