## Body
Three reasons your content isn't compounding:

1. **You're posting once, never reusing.** One video → one post → archive. The same insight could fuel 5 posts, an email, a community thread, a podcast clip.
2. **No CTA back to a community.** Posts that ask viewers to "follow for more" leak attention. Posts that drive them to a free resource convert attention into a relationship.
3. **No data on what's working.** Without tracking which hooks land, you keep guessing.
